The pipeline

You tap one shortcut after a shift → screenshots land in the shared Google Drive folder → a nightly job reads them, writes the day's spreadsheet, and updates this page. Nobody types numbers.

1 · Capture

Shift Snap walks you app by app, grabs each earnings screen, asks for the odometer, and saves everything to Runner Board / 01 Inbox.

2 · Extract

Nightly, the job reads every new file (OCR text first, image if needed) and pulls base pay, tips, fees, time, deliveries and miles per app.

3 · Spreadsheet

One Google Sheet per day in 02 Extracted and a CSV in the repo — the audit trail, with a confidence grade and source filename on every row.

4 · Publish

Rows are committed to the repo and this page re-reads them. Files move to 03 Processed; anything unreadable to 04 Needs Review.

The daily loop

  1. Before logging in — glance at the heatmap for today's weekday and the next block; open the app that paid best there.
  2. Borderline offer — "Should I take it?" tab, three numbers, verdict. Run Offer Snap to record it.
  3. End of shift — one tap on Shift Snap. Two minutes, app by app.
  4. Fuel or gear — photograph the receipt with Receipt Snap.
  5. Overnight — the job extracts, writes the day's sheet, and updates this page by morning.

Rules the job follows

  • Filename tells it runner, app and date; the META file supplies odometer miles and fees. Without META, miles come from the app screen or are flagged no odometer.
  • Same runner + app + date + total already loaded → skipped, not doubled.
  • Blurry, cropped, or totals that don't add up → 04 Needs Review, never guessed.
